Ultralight Geospatial LLC ("ULGS," "we," "us") builds offline-first field tools. This page describes what our mobile apps and this website do with information, and the choices you have. It applies to every version of every app we publish — free, limited, and paid alike.

WE OPERATE NO SERVERS. NOTHING YOU RECORD IS COLLECTED OR STORED BY US — YOUR RECORDS LIVE ON YOUR DEVICE.

Our Apps

This section applies to every mobile application published by Ultralight Geospatial LLC. Where apps differ, the difference is stated here by name. If an app is ever governed by a separate policy instead, it will be named here, and that app's store listing will link to its own policy — the policy linked from an app's store listing is always the one that governs that app. As of the effective date above, no such app exists: this page is the complete privacy policy for every ULGS app.

Location

Recording your device's GPS position — points, tracks, photo positions, elevation profiles — is what these tools are for. Location is used only while you are actively using an app to record or navigate, or while a recording you started is running. A recording you start continues while your screen is off or locked, shown by a visible ongoing notification; stopping the recording ends it. Position data is stored locally in the app's private storage.

Position fixes and Google's network location. On standard Android, if an app is allowed to use network-based location, the operating system may resolve your position through Google's network location service — meaning nearby cell and Wi-Fi information leaves your device to Google, by the platform, not by our code. Where our apps offer a GPS-only mode, choosing it means the app requests satellite positioning only; where network location is used at all, it is as a fallback when GPS has no fix. On de-Googled Android systems this platform service behaves according to that system's own configuration.

Your records

Waypoints, tracks, logs, routes, notes, and photos you create stay on your device. We cannot see, access, retrieve, or delete them. What happens to them if you uninstall an app depends on where that app keeps them:

  • Tracks, routes, logs, and settings (all apps): removed when you uninstall the app. Records you have not exported are gone with it.
  • Files you exported (all apps): yours, saved to your device's shared storage (such as Downloads), and they remain after uninstall — the app no longer owns them; you do.
  • Photos (camera app): saved to your device's photo gallery, and they remain after uninstall like any other photos. Manage or delete them in your gallery.

Device backup and phone-to-phone transfer are governed by your device's own backup and migration settings.

Platforms, today and later

Our apps currently run on Android phones and tablets, and this policy describes that architecture. We are interested in other form factors — watches, vehicle systems, and sharing between nearby devices. If we ship for them, some involve pathways this policy doesn't yet describe: syncing between a phone and a watch, for example, can pass through the platform's own services, the way network location does today. Our commitment is the same one this whole page rests on: any new data pathway will be described here, by name, before the version that uses it is released — and the promises that don't depend on architecture (no ULGS servers, no collection or storage by us, no selling, no ads, no accounts) hold on every platform we ever ship for.

Online map & elevation services

Some of our apps can fetch public map and terrain data over the network. When they do, the request goes directly from your device to the public service, under that service's own privacy policy. We are not in the middle and receive nothing. The services our apps contact, by name:

  • USGS 3DEP (elevation.nationalmap.gov) — elevation samples and terrain imagery
  • Esri public ArcGIS services — hillshade and slope imagery
  • Oregon DOGAMI and Washington DNR — state lidar and terrain layers
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass — map feature data
  • USGS / USFS — downloadable map sheets

Be aware of what these requests contain: fetching a map sheet or terrain layer sends the area you are asking about; sampling elevation along a route you have drawn sends that route's coordinates. Nothing about you — no name, account, or identifier we control — accompanies these requests, and we store nothing; but the request itself necessarily tells that service where you are looking or the line you are tracing, and their servers keep ordinary logs. Fetched data is cached in the app's private storage (bounded, clearable in the app, removed on uninstall) so areas you have loaded keep working offline. If you prefer that no request leave your device in the field, load your maps and terrain at home and work from the cache — the apps are built for exactly that.

Photos

In our camera app, location embedding is under your control, and photos can carry no location at all. Beyond that, the two copies the app can produce are deliberately separate in what they carry: the located copy contains position data and no camera metadata; the clean copy contains the camera's ordinary technical metadata — make, model, and firmware version, which describe the phone model and its software, not you or your individual device — and no location. No file the app produces contains both a position and a device description. Neither copy contains serial numbers or any per-device identifier.

Crash reports

If an app offers to send a crash report, the report is a file written on your device. It contains technical state — not your records or location history — and it is shown to you in full before anything is sent. Sending is always your action, by email, and never automatic. Anything you type into the optional note is included as you wrote it, so describe the problem in whatever detail you're comfortable sharing.

Permissions

Each app requests only the permissions its own features require, drawn from this set: Location (recording and navigation), Camera (photo capture), Storage/media (importing maps, exporting your data), and Notifications (recording status). No app requests all of them — a camera tool has no need of track notifications, and a profile tool has no need of your camera. Decline or revoke any permission in your device settings; the corresponding feature stops and the rest of the app carries on.

Purchases, free versions, and paid versions

Paid licenses and upgrades are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive transaction records (what was purchased, when, in what country) but never your payment card details.

Where we offer a free or limited version of an app, this same policy applies without exception: no advertising, no trackers, no accounts, and no additional data practices of any kind. A free version is a smaller tool, not a data funnel. Our apps do not phone home to check licenses, and we do not restrict your ability to export your own data in any version.

This Website

ulgeospatial.com is informational. No accounts, no advertising, no analytics scripts, no cookies set by us.

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard access logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) to operate and secure the site.
  • Fonts. Pages load a typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests a file from Google's servers; Google's privacy policy governs that request.

Your Rights

Because your records live on your device and not on our servers, you exercise most rights directly: view, export, and delete your data in the app at any time.

For what little we do hold — support email and store transaction records — you may request access, correction, or deletion at support@ulgeospatial.com. Residents of California, the EU/EEA, the UK, and other jurisdictions with privacy statutes may have additional rights under those laws; we honor valid requests regardless of where you live, and we do not discriminate against anyone for exercising a privacy right.

Security

Your records sit in app-private storage (or, for photos and exports, your device's own gallery and Downloads), protected by your device's security. Because we do not hold your data, the measures that matter are on your device: use device encryption and a strong screen lock, and follow your organization's device policies where they apply.

If your agency needs data-handling documentation for procurement review, write to support@ulgeospatial.com.
